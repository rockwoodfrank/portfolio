I inherited my family's old Volvo XC90 V8 with about 230,000 miles 
on it(mostly highway, thankfully). I'm convinced it still has a lot of life left in it,
despite the spotty repair schedule.

I have had some issues, however, and have been trying to make repairs to increase its life
for as long as possible. 

There are lots of great repair tutorials across the internet, but they're often hard to 
find or not perfectly specific.

Thus, this page is my attempt to curate all of the guides, tools, and resources that I found
incredibly helpful when getting this car to work again.

**DISCLAIMER: I am not a mechanic. My experience working on cars is limited. 
Additionally, these resources may not be perfectly accurate, they 
were just helpful for me. If you're unsure about something, talk to a mechanic first.**

## Online Resources
First of all, the SwedeSpeed forums is a community full of people with experience with Volvos.
The forum for the 1st Edition XC90:
- <NewTabLink href="https://www.swedespeed.com/forums/xc90-2003-2014.27/">XC90(2003 - 2014) | SwedeSpeed</NewTabLink> 

Is a great place to start when looking at repairs. Many of the guides I've linked here for reference.

For parts, IPD has great listings for OEM and aftermarket:
- <NewTabLink href="https://www.ipdusa.com/">www.ipdusa.com</NewTabLink>

In addition, check your local dealership to see if you can pick up parts there:
- <NewTabLink href="https://usparts.volvocars.com/">usparts.volvocars.com</NewTabLink>

I've found that local dealerships will usually sell parts below MSRP. I will 
always prefer to buy OEM because I got scammed when buying aftermarket coils,
and it feels like the safer option to just purchase parts from a reputable location.

## Tools
### V-Tool
Volvo's official tool for diagnosis and modifications is VIDA, which seems great but getting a license
as a non-mechanic has been difficult. Instead, what I've found to be incredibly helpful is V-Tool:
- <NewTabLink href="https://v-tool.app/">v-tool.app</NewTabLink>

Which can be downloaded from the Google or iOS App Store.
The main features are free, but you have to pay a one-time fee for more advanced functionality
such as resetting the transmission counter.

To interface with the app, you'll need a bluetooth OBD adapter. Only certain kinds will work, so
check their website for their recommendations.

### OBD Adapter
Personally, I bought a **vLinker FD+** because it was recommended by V-Tool and it might have
been the cheapest option. It's been working great so far. You can buy it on Amazon at the time of 
writing this.

## Diagnoses
### "Slow Down or Shift Up Message"
This happened to me, accompanied by a flashing Check Engine light:

<YoutubeVideo link="https://www.youtube.com/embed/LHcMvfoOD6U?si=QCv6Lcme3-vsV4hG" orientation="horizontal" />

A mechanic diagnosed that 6 of the 8 ignition coils were behaving improperly, so I replaced 6 coils instead 
of just the one like in the video.

I bought OEM coils, I got scammed with the first set of aftermarket coils that I bought and decided not to risk it 
again.

### Transmission Fluid Flush
Before I did the repair the transmission fluid had *never* been flushed. I noticed that I had some trouble
shifting, and decided that I had better replace it before things got worse. I had heard rumors online that
flushing the fluid could break the transmission after this much time(I have no idea if this true), but 
I figured that it was better for it to break down at home rather than halfway through a long road trip.

- <NewTabLink href="https://www.swedespeed.com/threads/how-to-xc90-6-speed-transmission-fluid-flush.204760/">
    How To: XC90 6-speed transmission fluid flush
    </NewTabLink>

This tutorial was great. I bought fluid from the dealership since I didn't want to add any uncertainty.

Be super precise with the fluid level, I originally added too much and I got a "Low Oil Level" message
after driving for a mile or two(yes, a low oil message for too much oil), so then I took out too much,
and finally added the right amount back. The V-Tool app would have been incredibly helpful for getting 
the right temperature for setting at the time.
